# BlueQuiz product design

BlueQuiz is an offline-first study tool. The primary flow is: load a saved set, configure a short practice session, answer, review mistakes, and repeat.

Priorities:

1. Prevent invalid question and answer data from entering a quiz.
2. Keep frequent study actions visible and advanced authoring controls secondary.
3. Preserve progress locally and make it exportable.
4. Keep the app usable from local files without a build step or internet connection.
